::: challenge

<OneColumn title="What is the challenge?">
<template #plain>

Serious games serve as a powerful tool for harnessing collective intelligence, solving problems, and defining actions. These games are accessible and easy to play. iObeya is utilized as a presentation tool, with guided meetings, but it falls short in managing creative animations and activities.

The challenge lies in efficiently enabling collective intelligence.

</template>
</OneColumn>

:::

::: section

<OneColumn title="Divergence-Convergence as a tool to enable collective intelligence">
<template #plain>

Facilitators use brainstorming to stimulate creativity and voting to achieve results. Brainstorming allows individuals to jot down and share their ideas, while voting aids in selecting the most suitable one. This combination stems from the Double Diamonds model, where the essence lies in the Divergence-Convergence process.

</template>
</OneColumn>

To facilitate collective intelligence and enhance creativity, we hypothesize that employing animation and illustrations in the user interface can guide participants toward a certain mindset.

::: success

<OneColumn title="Go further…">
<template #plain>

Compared to our competitors (Klaxoon and Mural), iObeya could benefit from several improvements:

- Allow external participants to access modes (the whiteboarding session is one possible solution).
- Encourage idea generation (through drawings, pictures, rounds, etc.).
- Maintain awareness of the activity context.
- Facilitate smoother interactions between the facilitator and participants.

Stay tuned!

</template>
